# Diamond Operator Manual

This manual is written for the person using Diamond, not for the person coding it.

Diamond is a social media operating tool. Its job is to help you plan posts, write drafts, manage brands, open the correct social account, stage posts in a browser, keep proof of what happened, schedule work, and track results. It should not quietly publish something when the account, brand, license, proof, or content is unclear.

The most important rule is simple: Diamond helps you get a post ready, but you stay in control of final publishing until the product has been proven much more deeply.

## 1. The Basic Diamond Workflow

Use this workflow whenever you are unsure what to do next.

1. Open Posts.
2. Pick or create a post package.
3. Choose the company, brand, campaign, and platform.
4. Read the platform draft.
5. Evaluate the draft.
6. Approve the draft if it is good.
7. Add media if the platform needs media.
8. Stage the draft in the browser.
9. Review the social media composer yourself.
10. Publish manually on the social media site.
11. Capture proof in Diamond.
12. Mark the draft posted.
13. Check Analytics later.

Example:

You want to post a World Cup signup post to X.

1. Go to Posts.
2. Open the World Cup post package.
3. Confirm the platform draft says X.
4. Click Evaluate.
5. If Diamond says the draft is safe, click Approve.
6. Click Stage in browser.
7. Look at the X composer.
8. If the post looks right, publish it yourself in X.
9. Return to Diamond and click Capture Proof.
10. Click Mark Posted.

## 2. Words Diamond Uses

### Company

A company is the customer or product owner. For The Card, the company is `thecard.bet`.

Example:

If Diamond is being used for another company later, that company should have its own company record. Do not post The Card content from another company's browser profile.

### Brand

A brand belongs to a company. The Card is a brand under `thecard.bet`.

Example:

Company: `thecard.bet`
Brand: `The Card`
Campaign: `World Cup 2026`

### Campaign

A campaign is a focused marketing push.

Examples:

- World Cup 2026
- Founder/investor campaign
- Product launch
- Free league signup push

### Post Package

A post package is one source idea with one or more platform drafts.

Example:

Source idea:

> Join the free World Cup league and see where your country lands on the leaderboard.

Platform drafts:

- X version
- Instagram caption
- TikTok caption
- LinkedIn version
- Facebook version

### Platform Draft

A platform draft is the actual post text for one social platform.

Example:

The X draft should be shorter and sharper. The LinkedIn draft can be longer. TikTok might need a hook and video caption.

### Stage

Stage means Diamond opens the social platform and tries to put the post into the composer, or prepares you to do it manually.

Staging does not mean publish.

### Proof

Proof is evidence that the workflow happened.

Examples:

- A screenshot of the staged composer
- A URL of a published post
- A note that media upload was completed
- A record that the account was logged in

### Fail Closed

Fail closed means Diamond stops instead of guessing.

Example:

If Diamond is not sure whether you are logged into the correct X account, it should stop and ask you to fix the account instead of staging the post into the wrong place.

## 3. Posts Page

The Posts page is the main daily workspace.

Use it to:

- See draft posts
- See scheduled posts
- See published posts
- See failed or blocked work
- Open post packages
- Create new posts

### How To Read The Board

Common columns or statuses:

- Draft: not approved yet
- Needs review: Diamond found something that needs human judgment
- Scheduled: planned for later
- Staged: prepared in a social platform composer
- Published/Posted: already published
- Failed: something went wrong

Example:

If you see three failed posts and ten drafts, look at Failed first. A failed post may mean the account is wrong, media is missing, or a platform changed its page.

### Create A New Post Package

Use Create when you need a new post idea.

Example:

1. Click Create.
2. Enter the idea:

   > Free World Cup league is open. $1,000 total payouts. Pick matches and climb the country leaderboard.

3. Add platform drafts for X and Facebook.
4. Evaluate each draft.
5. Approve only the drafts that are ready.

### Open An Existing Post Package

Click a post card to open its details.

Use this when:

- You want to edit the post idea
- You want to review platform versions
- You want to add media
- You want to schedule or stage the post

## 4. Post Detail View

The post detail view is where the actual work happens.

You will usually see:

- Source idea
- Platform draft cards
- Media tools
- Evaluation results
- Approval buttons
- Staging tools
- Proof tools

### Evaluate

Evaluate checks the draft before you approve or stage it.

It may check:

- Risky language
- Prize claims
- Gambling-like wording
- Legal or regulatory language
- Repetition
- Quality
- Missing media
- Brand fit

Example:

Draft:

> Guaranteed winnings for anyone who joins.

Diamond should block or flag this because "guaranteed winnings" is risky and probably false.

Better draft:

> Join the free World Cup league, make your picks, and climb the leaderboard.

### Approve

Approve means you accept the draft for scheduling or staging.

Do not approve a draft just because it exists. Approve only after reading it.

Example:

Approve this:

> Free World Cup league is open. Make your picks and see where your country lands.

Do not approve this without review:

> Win money instantly with our World Cup betting system.

### Stage In Browser

Stage in browser opens the platform composer or platform page.

For X, Diamond may be able to insert the draft text.

For other platforms, Diamond may open the right page and copy text for you, but you may still need to paste or upload manually.

Example for X:

1. Approve the X draft.
2. Click Stage in browser.
3. Diamond opens X compose.
4. Diamond attempts to insert the text.
5. You review it.
6. You publish manually.

Example for Instagram:

1. Approve the Instagram draft.
2. Add the image or video.
3. Click Stage in browser.
4. Diamond opens Instagram.
5. You use Instagram's Create flow.
6. Paste the caption if needed.
7. Upload the image or video manually if needed.
8. Publish manually.
9. Capture proof in Diamond.

### Upload Media

Upload media attaches a media path to the draft.

Use this for:

- Instagram posts
- TikTok videos
- YouTube Shorts
- Any post that needs a card, image, screenshot, or video

Example:

If an Instagram post says "media required," add the World Cup leaderboard image before staging.

### Capture Proof

Capture Proof records evidence that the workflow happened.

Use it after:

- Staging a composer
- Uploading media
- Publishing manually
- Confirming login/session

Proof kinds:

- `account_session`: proof that the account is logged in
- `staged_composer`: proof that the draft reached the composer
- `manual_upload`: proof that media was uploaded manually
- `screenshot`: proof using a screenshot
- `published_post`: proof that the post is live

Example:

You publish a Facebook post manually.

1. Copy the live Facebook post URL.
2. Return to Diamond.
3. Click Capture Proof.
4. Use proof kind `published_post`.
5. Click Mark Posted.

### Mark Posted

Mark Posted tells Diamond the post is live.

Use it only after the post is actually published on the social platform.

Example:

If the X composer is open but you have not clicked Post inside X, do not click Mark Posted yet.

### Mark Abandoned

Mark Abandoned means you are intentionally not using that draft.

Use it when:

- The draft is no longer relevant
- The campaign changed
- The post is duplicated
- The platform version is bad and should not be used

Example:

You have two drafts saying nearly the same thing. Keep the stronger one and mark the weaker one abandoned.

## 5. Calendar

Calendar is for planned work.

Use it to:

- See what is overdue
- See what is due today
- See upcoming posts
- Load a scheduled draft
- Stage a scheduled post
- Mark a scheduled post posted
- Cancel a schedule

### Schedule A Post

Example:

You want a World Cup leaderboard post to go out tomorrow morning.

1. Open the post package.
2. Approve the platform draft.
3. Click Schedule post.
4. Choose tomorrow morning.
5. Check Calendar to confirm it appears under Upcoming.

### Load A Scheduled Post

Use this when it is time to publish.

Example:

1. Go to Calendar.
2. Find Ready Today.
3. Click the scheduled item.
4. Load the draft.
5. Stage it in browser.
6. Publish manually.
7. Capture proof.
8. Mark posted.

### Cancel A Scheduled Post

Cancel if the post should not go out.

Example:

A match changed, a prize detail changed, or the campaign message is no longer accurate.

## 6. Accounts

Accounts is where Diamond tracks social accounts, browser profiles, and session status.

Use it to:

- Add social accounts
- Open login pages
- Check whether a session is ready
- Keep accounts separated by company and brand
- Capture account proof

### Add A Social Account

Example:

To add TikTok for The Card:

1. Go to Accounts.
2. Click Add Account.
3. Choose company `thecard.bet`.
4. Choose brand `The Card`.
5. Choose platform `TikTok`.
6. Enter the account URL.
7. Save.

### Check Session

Check Session means Diamond is checking whether this account looks ready to use.

Example:

If X is logged out, the session is not ready. Open the account, log in manually, then mark/check the session again.

### Browser Profiles

Browser profiles keep accounts separated.

Example:

The Card X account should not share a browser profile with another company's X account.

This matters because browser profiles preserve login sessions.

### Account Proof

Account proof shows that a login or setup step was completed.

Example:

After you log into Facebook successfully, capture `account_session` proof.

## 7. Brands

Brands is where Diamond keeps the identity and strategy for the company.

Use it to edit:

- Company name
- Brand name
- Campaign name
- Goals
- Audience
- Pillars
- Voice
- Approved phrases
- Banned phrases
- Claims and prize language

### Goals

Goals tell Diamond what the campaign is trying to do.

Example:

> Drive free World Cup league signups.

### Audience

Audience tells Diamond who the post is for.

Example:

> World Cup fans who like picks, brackets, country pride, and leaderboard competition.

### Pillars

Pillars are repeatable content themes.

Examples:

- Country pride
- Leaderboard movement
- Matchday urgency
- Prize clarity
- Free-to-play onboarding

### Voice

Voice tells Diamond how the brand should sound.

Example:

Good:

> Plainspoken, sports-smart, direct, and useful.

Bad:

> Overhyped, casino-like, or full of fake urgency.

### Approved Phrases

Approved phrases are safe phrases Diamond can reuse.

Examples:

- free World Cup league
- make your picks
- climb the leaderboard
- thecard.bet

### Banned Phrases

Banned phrases should trigger review or blocking.

Examples:

- guaranteed win
- risk-free money
- sportsbook
- betting system

## 9. Settings

Settings is the control room.

Use Settings for:

- License
- Firebase sync
- Routine timing
- Themes
- Accessibility
- Legal drafts
- User guide
- Expert checklist
- Auto-publish gate

### Save Settings

Use Save Settings after editing settings fields.

Example:

If you change the routine due window from 15 minutes to 60 minutes, click Save Settings.

### Check Firebase

Check Firebase tells you whether Diamond can see the Firebase admin configuration.

Example:

Use this if license sync or Firestore export seems broken.

### Sync License

Sync License checks the Diamond license record.

Right now there is a temporary unlimited license until the shop is ready.

Example:

If Diamond says a brand or platform is not licensed, click Sync License, then read the status.

### Export Firestore Bundle

Export Firestore Bundle creates a backend-ready data bundle.

Use it when:

- You want to inspect Diamond's data
- You want to sync with Firebase later
- You need a support/debug snapshot

### Copy Legal Summary

Copies the current legal draft summaries.

Use it when reviewing Terms, Privacy, or acceptable use language.

### Copy Expert Checklist

Copies the internal product-readiness checklist.

Use it to see whether Diamond thinks the operating layers are ready.

### Copy Auto-Publish Decision

Copies why auto-publish is locked or ready.

For now, expect it to be locked.

### Copy User Guide

Copies the in-app guide.

Use it when you want a quick workflow reminder.

### Start Walkthrough

Starts the screen tour.

Use it when you want Diamond to highlight the major areas of the app.

## 10. Operator

Operator is the advanced control panel.

Use Operator when:

- You need to stage a post in the browser
- You need to check session state
- You need to capture proof
- You need to validate a post package
- You need to export sync data
- You need to inspect the proof queue

### Active Target

Active Target shows what Diamond thinks you are working on.

Check this before staging.

Example:

If Active Target says:

Company: `thecard.bet`
Brand: `The Card`
Platform: `X`

then it is safe to continue only if that is what you intended.

### Preflight Checks

Preflight checks look for blockers before staging.

Examples:

- Is the license valid?
- Is Firebase configured?
- Is the account session ready?
- Is there an active draft?
- Is the draft approved?
- Is cadence allowed?

If preflight is red, stop and fix the issue.

### Open Account

Opens the login or account page.

Example:

Use Open Account when TikTok says the session is unknown. Log in manually, then return to Diamond.

### Check Session

Marks or checks whether the session is ready.

Example:

After logging into Facebook, click Check Session so Diamond knows the account is usable.

### Stage In Browser

Stages the active draft in the platform browser flow.

Example:

For X, Diamond may insert the text.

For Instagram, Diamond may open the page and copy the caption, but you may need to upload and paste manually.

### Capture Proof

Records proof for the active draft/account.

Example:

After you see the staged composer on X, capture proof.

### Validate Package

Checks the active package for readiness.

Use it before staging if you are unsure.

Example:

If Validate Package says media is missing for TikTok, add video media before staging.

### Copy Proof Queue

Copies a list of proof gaps for all platforms.

Example:

It might say:

- X: needs 2 more staging sessions
- Instagram: needs media upload proof
- Facebook: needs manual staging proof

## 11. Proof Queue

The proof queue tells you which platforms have enough evidence to be trusted.

Proof types:

- Login proof: account can be accessed
- Text proof: Diamond can insert text
- Media proof: Diamond can attach or confirm media
- Manual proof: human completed the platform workflow
- Staging sessions: repeated successful staging across app sessions

Example:

If X says `Text proof 1/3`, Diamond needs two more successful text insertion proofs before that part is considered proven.

Example:

If Instagram says `Media proof 0/1`, publish or stage an Instagram post with media and capture `manual_upload` proof.

## 15. Auto-Publish

Auto-publish is locked by default.

That means Diamond should not publish by itself.

Auto-publish can only become ready if all of these are true:

- Policy is explicitly enabled
- Scott workflow signoff exists
- Platform proof is complete
- Automation license allows it
- Draft approval and quality gates pass

For now, treat auto-publish as a future concept, not a current workflow.

## 16. Common Problems

### Problem: Diamond says session unknown

What it means:

Diamond does not know whether the social account is logged in.

What to do:

1. Go to Accounts or Operator.
2. Open Account.
3. Log in manually.
4. Return to Diamond.
5. Click Check Session.
6. Capture account proof if needed.

### Problem: Media required

What it means:

The platform probably needs an image or video.

What to do:

1. Add media to the draft.
2. Confirm the media path is visible.
3. Stage again.

### Problem: Review required

What it means:

Diamond found language that needs human judgment.

What to do:

1. Read the risk reason.
2. Edit the draft.
3. Evaluate again.
4. Approve only if it is accurate and safe.

### Problem: Browser opened but text did not appear

What it means:

The platform may have changed its composer or the adapter is not proven.

What to do:

1. Copy the draft text.
2. Paste manually into the composer.
3. Publish manually if correct.
4. Capture proof.
5. Leave the proof queue open so the platform remains marked as not fully proven.

### Problem: Wrong account

What it means:

You may be in the wrong browser profile or social account.

What to do:

1. Stop.
2. Do not publish.
3. Go to Accounts.
4. Confirm company, brand, platform, handle, and browser profile.
5. Open the correct account.

### Problem: License blocked

What it means:

Diamond thinks this brand, platform, or automation action is not licensed.

What to do:

1. Go to Settings.
2. Click Sync License.
3. Read the license status.
4. Use the temporary unlimited license until the shop/license portal is ready.

## 17. Safe Daily Operating Routine

Use this routine when running Diamond for a campaign.

1. Open Diamond.
2. Check Posts for failed or review-needed items.
3. Check Calendar for ready posts.
4. Check Accounts if any platform session is unknown.
5. Open the highest-priority scheduled post.
6. Evaluate and approve.
7. Stage in browser.
8. Publish manually.
9. Capture proof.
10. Mark posted.
11. Check Analytics later.
12. Add notes if performance was unusual.

## 18. What Not To Do

Do not publish without reading the final social platform composer.

Do not use the wrong company or brand.

Do not ignore review-required language around money, prizes, gambling, legal, investment, equity, or support promises.

Do not mark posted before the post is live.

Do not treat candidate automation as proven automation.

Do not use auto-publish until Diamond has real proof, signoff, and licensing controls.

## 19. Simple Glossary

Approved: A human accepted the draft.

Blocked: Diamond found something that should not proceed.

Candidate adapter: Diamond has a possible way to help on a platform, but it has not been proven yet.

Draft: Text that has not been published.

Fail closed: Stop instead of guessing.

Manual upload: You upload the media yourself on the platform.

Platform: X, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube Shorts, Facebook, or Reddit.

Proof: Evidence that something happened.

Schedule: A planned time for a post.

Stage: Prepare the post in the platform composer.

## 20. The One-Page Version

When in doubt:

1. Confirm the active company and brand.
2. Confirm the social account.
3. Evaluate the draft.
4. Approve the draft.
5. Add required media.
6. Stage in browser.
7. Review the composer yourself.
8. Publish manually.
9. Capture proof.
10. Mark posted.
